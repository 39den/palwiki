Salvage · To salvage in Palworld, mount a Water-type Pal, equip a Fishing Rod, and interact with Floating Salvage Barrels in the ocean. Complete the fishing minigame to collect materials like Coralum Ore and rare schematics.

Basic Salvaging · Any Fishing Rod and a Water mount.

Beginner Fishing Rod (Chillet) · Intermediate Fishing Rod (Cattiva) · Ride your Pal in the ocean, find a wooden barrel (basic salvage), aim, and cast your rod.

Minigame: Successfully keep the moving green bar behind the fish/salvage icon until the circle fills up.

Advanced Salvaging · Powerful Fishing Rod, a Powerful Fishing Magnet in your inventory, and a Water Pal.

Advanced Fishing Rod (Pengullet) · Advanced Fishing Rod (Depresso) · Search the high-level seas around Sakurajima Island or Feybreak Island.

Look for barrels with gold-colored metal bands.Cost: Using advanced salvage consumes 1 Fishing Magnet per successful pull.

Tips for Better Loot · Use Pals like Jellroy as your active mount; their partner skills give you a chance to double your loot or get rare items.
